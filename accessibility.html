Accessibility

The site is designed to support keyboard navigation, readable text, meaningful image descriptions and reduced-motion preferences.

Using the site

  • Use Tab to move through links and controls.
  • Use Enter or Space to activate menu-category buttons.
  • Use Escape to close mobile navigation.
  • Browser zoom and your device’s reduced-motion setting are supported.

Need another way to order?

If any part of the website is difficult to use, call (718) 746-4370 for restaurant and ordering assistance.

Third-party ordering and mapping services control their own websites. These features are not a legal certification of accessibility.
